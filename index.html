Online exposure has slowed

I know a price conversation can feel discouraging. The professional photography, listing preparation, and video and social content we produced put this home in front of thousands of people, and online exposure has been okay. But it has not held steady since the home was listed; it has declined. Fewer buyers are reaching the listing each day, and that attention is not yet turning into showings. We had no showings this week and therefore no new buyer feedback to share.

How Zillow buyers are responding

The listing has generated 917 Zillow views and 65 saves. About 7.1% of viewers have saved the property, roughly one out of every 14 visitors. That is reasonably close to Tempe, a highly comparable nearby listing at 8.1%, though both trail Hannibal at 10.8% and fall below the 10% level I look for in a listing that is building real momentum.

The larger gap is traffic. Pitkin is averaging 15.5 Zillow views per day, compared with 38.2 for Tempe and 46.8 for Hannibal. The buyers who find our home are engaging at a comparable rate; fewer buyers are finding it each day. Our focus is on converting the buyers who see the home and bringing more qualified buyers to it.

How we compare with 6063 S Tempe Way

Tempe is the home buyers are most likely to weigh against ours, and it has real advantages. It was built in 1998, making it newer than our home. It sits in Saddle Rock Ridge, a more desirable neighborhood close to Southlands. Its yard does not need any work, and its vaulted-ceiling great room is a significant difference buyers notice the moment they walk in.

Even with those advantages, and a price only $5,900 above ours, Tempe has now been on the market for 26 days. A buyer comparing the two at nearly the same price is likely to choose Tempe. Our price needs to give buyers a clear reason to choose our home instead.

Why the rate environment matters

The recent rise in mortgage rates has been meaningful. Freddie Mac reported that the national average 30-year fixed mortgage rate reached 7.03% on September 24, up from 6.95% one week earlier and 6.66% on August 27. Individual loan quotes will vary, but buyers in this price range are often very payment-sensitive. When rates move higher quickly, buyers may reduce their price ceiling, delay showings, or become more selective about which homes they tour.

Source: Freddie Mac Primary Mortgage Market Survey, September 24, 2026

Relaunching the listing as new

Along with the price change, I plan to let the current listing expire and re-list it the next day. That resets the home's days on market and presents it to buyers as a brand new listing, which puts it back into new-listing alerts and in front of buyers who may have scrolled past it. Newer listings like Tempe are drawing far more daily traffic than we are, and a fresh listing date helps close that gap.

I want to be upfront with you: I will be formally reprimanded and fined for doing this. I only do it once a year to avoid greater consequences, and I am choosing to take that penalty on myself as an investment in selling your home.

To make the relaunch feel new at first glance, I will switch the lead photo and add new photos featuring the rocks and the stained deck in front.

When we first started talking about pricing, $560,000 was more in line with the price point we were hoping for, and I know a move to $560,000 would feel much easier to accept. You invested $16,000 getting the home ready, and it really is in much better shape because of it. Then the market turned in a way none of us expected when interest rates moved up.

My honest opinion is that we may not be able to sell this home for more than $550,000 in today's market. The market is really tough right now. The buyers I am working with are backing off and lowering the price ranges they search in. Moving to $560,000 would not be a meaningful difference to them.

Why not start at $560,000?

A move to $560,000 probably feels better, and I understand why. Realistically, though, I think we are going to end up at $550,000. If we step down to $560,000 first, we spend the relaunch at a price that keeps us in front of the same buyers who have already passed, and we likely face a second reduction a few weeks later with less momentum behind it.

A more assertive drop now gives us our best shot. Paired with the re-list, the home comes back as a new listing at a new price point, in front of a new group of buyers, at the moment it has the most attention.

$550,000 puts your home in a new search range. Zillow price searches run in $25,000 to $50,000 increments. At $550,000, the home appears for a whole group of buyers searching a lower price range who are not seeing it today, and paired with the relaunch, that can bring a wave of new attention.

I recognize this is a bigger step than we planned, and the decision is yours. I would rather recommend the move I believe will sell your home than a smaller one that leaves us in the same position a few weeks from now.

Next week we will be watching Zillow views per day and save rate against the same competitors, and whether showing requests return after the relaunch.

What the comparison tells us

The closest four-bedroom closed comparison is 5482 S Salida Street at $570,000, but it has more finished square footage. Another nearby home closed at $560,000, while a similar-sized four-bedroom listing was withdrawn at $559,000. A pending sale at $599,000 confirms that buyers are still making decisions, but they are being selective. Combined with slowing online traffic and no showings this week, the pattern suggests the home needs both more buyer reach and a sharper value message.

Why waiting for spring is not guaranteed to help

Spring often brings more buyers, but it also usually brings more competing listings. If mortgage rates remain elevated or buyer confidence softens further, the seasonal increase in activity may not produce a better result. I do not want to create unnecessary urgency, but I also do not think we should assume that waiting will automatically improve our position. Acting now with a meaningful adjustment and a relaunch gives us the best chance to create new traffic.
