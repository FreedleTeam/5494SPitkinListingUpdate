Where You're Losing

Buyers Are Seeing a Project

We put in as much updating as the budget allowed, and it made a real difference. But buyers at this price are looking for turnkey, and they're still walking through your home and seeing work ahead of them. Three things keep coming up:

The primary bathroom layout. The sink sits out in the bedroom rather than enclosed with the rest of the bathroom. It's the kind of thing a buyer notices right away and starts mentally pricing a remodel.

Updating overall. Compared to the most updated homes in this group, the finishes still read as partially done. Buyers comparing side by side are noticing the difference.

The landscaping. The large lot is an asset on paper, but right now it reads as a yard that needs work. Several competing homes are marketing finished, ready-to-use outdoor space.

That's the honest gap. On space and price per square foot, we're ahead. On condition and layout, the most updated homes at $550,000 are ahead of us, and turnkey is what buyers in this range are paying for right now.

The Decision

Selling This Year vs. Waiting for Spring

This one is your call, and I want to give you my honest read on both paths.

Reduce the price now

  • I believe that to sell this year, the price needs to come down.
  • At $550,000, buyers are comparing you directly against homes like Waco that need no work. A lower price lets them account for the bathroom, the updating, and the yard and still feel good about the deal.
  • Selling now ends the carrying costs and takes the uncertainty about where prices go next off the table.

Hold and relaunch in spring

  • My hope is that $550,000 could be achievable next spring, when more buyers are typically out. I can't tell you whether that's realistic.
  • Nobody knows yet what spring will look like. If prices keep drifting down through winter, even an improving market may not get back to where we are today, and we don't know how much ground it would recover.

Whichever way you decide, I'm in this with you for as long as you are. If you want to sell this year, let's talk through the number together. If you'd rather wait for spring, we'll use the time to put the house in the strongest position we can for the relaunch.

What I'll be watching in the meantime: whether Waco goes under contract and how quickly, whether any of the homes sitting at $550,000 for 75 days or more find a buyer, and any change in showing activity on yours.
